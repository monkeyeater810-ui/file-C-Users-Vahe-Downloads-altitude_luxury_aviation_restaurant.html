Our Story

Built on Burbank's Legacy

Burbank is no ordinary city. Lockheed built the P-38 here. Amelia Earhart took flight from this valley. The Verdugo Hills watched as the aviation age was forged below — in hangars, on runways, by dreamers with grease on their hands and stars in their eyes.

Altair was conceived in that same spirit. Named for the brightest star in Aquila — the Eagle constellation — our restaurant sits on a hilltop above Hollywood Burbank Airport, offering unobstructed views of the very runways where history was made. We serve steaks and seasonally driven cuisine worthy of the view beneath you.
